### 1. Landing Page Structure & Components
- **Top Notice Bar**: Contact phone helpline (`044-24567890 / 1800-200-4567`), OPD hours, and clinic location.
- **Responsive Sticky Navigation**: Logo with medical cross, dynamic active link highlighting on scroll, and mobile navigation drawer.
- **Hero Section with CTA**: Clear medical headline, Free OPD highlights, key statistics counter (15+ Years, 50+ Doctors, 100k+ Patients), and emergency desk details.
- **Highlights Grid**: Specialist Doctors, Computerized Diagnostic Lab, 24x7 In-House Pharmacy, and Emergency Response.
- **About the Clinic**: Clinic history, NABH standards, modern facilities, preventive wellness, and ethical care values.
- **Medical Services Section**: 8 specialty clinical departments (General Medicine [Free OPD], Pediatrics [Charitable OPD], Cardiology, Dermatology, Orthopedics, Neurology, Ophthalmology, and ENT) with detailed bullet points.
- **Why Choose Us**: 6 patient-centric advantages (Experienced Specialists, Affordable & Free OPD options, Diagnostic Lab, Minimal Waiting Time, Sanitized Environment, 24x7 Emergency Desk).
- **Doctor Highlights**: Featured doctors with qualifications, years of experience, OPD room numbers, and consultation fees.
- **Patient Testimonials**: Authentic patient reviews with 5-star ratings.
- **Contact Information & Google Maps Integration**: Embedded responsive Google Maps iframe and complete hospital reception details.
- **Footer**: Comprehensive navigation, department links, hospital address, and copyright notices.

### 2. Appointment Enquiry Form & Validation
- **Patient Full Name**: Minimum 3 characters verification.
- **Email Address**: Regular expression format checking.
- **Phone Number**: 10-digit mobile number validation.
- **Preferred Department**: Selection requirement.
- **Medical Concern / Message**: Optional textarea for symptoms.
- **Submission Feedback**: Generates an Enquiry Reference Token (e.g. `ENQ-2026-8192`) and displays a confirmation dialog with `localStorage` persistence.
